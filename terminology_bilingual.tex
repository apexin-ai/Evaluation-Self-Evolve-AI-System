\section*{terminology\_bilingual}

The three role labels are survey-level functional labels. The English terms below
are the recommended mapping from the Chinese concepts used by the editors to the
paper vocabulary found across the literature.

\paragraph{Editorial rule.}
The papers may use different names, such as self-feedback, verbal reinforcement, evaluator-guided search, held-out evaluation, replay, or judge benchmarking. The mapping should therefore be stated explicitly rather than presented as a claim that all papers use the same role vocabulary. In every coding decision, keep the following order visible: target or evidence entity $\rightarrow$ judgment or property $\rightarrow$ downstream decision or progress claim.
